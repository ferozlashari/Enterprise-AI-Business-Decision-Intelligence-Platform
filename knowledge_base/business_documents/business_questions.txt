Common Enterprise AI Questions


Question:
Why did sales decrease?


Answer:
Sales decrease can happen because of lower customer demand, product performance issues, market changes or operational problems.


Question:
How can inventory shortage be prevented?


Answer:
Inventory shortage can be prevented using demand forecasting, safety stock calculation and optimized reorder points.


Question:
What is AI Copilot?


Answer:
AI Copilot is an intelligent assistant that combines enterprise data, machine learning predictions and business knowledge to answer executive questions.
