Enterprise AI Business Decision Intelligence Platform

The platform provides artificial intelligence based business intelligence solutions for enterprise organizations.

The system analyzes sales, inventory, customers and operational data to generate predictions, recommendations and executive decisions.

Core capabilities:

1. Sales Prediction
2. Demand Forecasting
3. Inventory Optimization
4. Customer Segmentation
5. AI Business Copilot
6. Executive Decision Support


The platform architecture includes:

- Machine Learning Models
- LangGraph Multi-Agent System
- Retrieval Augmented Generation
- FAISS Vector Database
- Groq Large Language Model


The objective is to help business leaders make data driven decisions.
