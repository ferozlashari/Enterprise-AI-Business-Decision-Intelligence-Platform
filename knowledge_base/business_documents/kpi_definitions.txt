Enterprise KPI Definitions


Sales KPI:

Revenue:
Total income generated from product sales.


Growth Rate:
Percentage change compared with previous period.


Profit Margin:
Profit generated after cost deduction.


Inventory KPI:

Inventory Demand:
Expected product requirement.


Safety Stock:
Additional inventory maintained to avoid shortage.


Reorder Point:
Stock level where new inventory should be ordered.


Customer KPI:

Customer Lifetime Value:
Expected value generated by a customer.


Customer Segment:
Group of customers with similar purchasing behavior.
