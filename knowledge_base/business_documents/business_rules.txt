Enterprise Business Rules


Sales Intelligence:

Sales performance is evaluated using historical sales trends, product performance, customer behavior and market conditions.


Inventory Intelligence:

Inventory decisions depend on:

- Demand forecast
- Current stock level
- Safety stock
- Reorder point
- Supplier lead time


Customer Intelligence:

Customers are segmented based on:

- Purchase frequency
- Spending behavior
- Customer value


Decision Engine:

The AI decision engine combines:

- Machine learning predictions
- Business rules
- Historical knowledge
- Risk analysis


Executive recommendations must include:

- Current situation
- Business risk
- Recommended action
- Expected impact
