Enterprise KPI Definitions


Sales KPIs:


Revenue:

Total sales income.


Profit:

Revenue minus operational cost.


Profit Margin:

Profit percentage compared with revenue.


Growth Rate:

Business improvement percentage.



Inventory KPIs:


Inventory Demand:

Expected product requirement.


Safety Stock:

Extra inventory protection.


Reorder Point:

Required stock level for new order.



Customer KPIs:


Customer Lifetime Value:

Expected customer revenue contribution.


Customer Segment:

Group of customers with similar behavior.
