Enterprise Decision Policies


AI recommendations must include:


Current Situation:

Explain the detected business condition.


Business Risk:

Identify possible negative impact.


Recommended Action:

Provide practical solution.


Expected Impact:

Explain possible business improvement.



Decision Examples:


Sales Decrease:

Risk:
Revenue reduction.

Action:
Analyze customer demand and improve product strategy.



Inventory Shortage:

Risk:
Lost sales opportunities.

Action:
Increase stock and optimize reorder point.



Customer Churn:

Risk:
Revenue loss.

Action:
Launch customer retention strategy.
