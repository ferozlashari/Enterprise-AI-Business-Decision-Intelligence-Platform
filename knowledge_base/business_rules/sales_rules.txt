Sales Intelligence Rules


Sales analysis evaluates business performance using:

- Historical sales
- Product performance
- Customer behavior
- Market conditions
- Seasonal trends


Sales KPIs:


Revenue:

Total income generated from product sales.


Sales Growth:

Percentage increase or decrease compared with previous period.


Average Order Value:

Average amount spent by customers per transaction.


Sales Decline Analysis:


When sales decrease, analyze:

1. Customer demand reduction

2. Product availability issues

3. Pricing changes

4. Competitor activities

5. Seasonal effects

6. Marketing performance


Recommended Actions:

- Improve marketing campaigns
- Optimize pricing
- Increase product availability
- Analyze customer requirements
