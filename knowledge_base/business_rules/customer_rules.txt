Customer Intelligence Rules


Customer analysis identifies customer behavior patterns.


Customer Segmentation:


High Value Customers:

Customers with:

- High spending
- Frequent purchases
- Strong engagement


Regular Customers:

Customers with stable purchasing behavior.


Low Value Customers:

Customers with limited purchases.


Churn Risk Customers:

Customers showing:

- Reduced activity
- Lower spending
- Long inactivity period


Recommended Actions:


High Value:

Provide loyalty programs.


Regular:

Increase engagement.


Low Value:

Provide targeted offers.


Churn Risk:

Launch retention campaigns.
