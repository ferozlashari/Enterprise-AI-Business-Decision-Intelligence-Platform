Inventory Intelligence Rules


Inventory management focuses on maintaining optimal stock levels.


Important Factors:

Demand Forecast:

Predict future product requirements.


Safety Stock:

Additional inventory maintained to avoid shortage.


Reorder Point:

Inventory level where new stock should be ordered.


Lead Time:

Time required from supplier order to inventory arrival.


Economic Order Quantity:

Optimal order quantity to minimize inventory cost.



Inventory Risks:


Stockout Risk:

Occurs when available inventory is insufficient for customer demand.


Overstock Risk:

Occurs when inventory exceeds customer requirements.



Recommended Actions:


For shortage:

- Increase inventory level
- Improve demand forecasting
- Adjust reorder point


For excess inventory:

- Reduce purchasing
- Run promotions
- Optimize supply planning
