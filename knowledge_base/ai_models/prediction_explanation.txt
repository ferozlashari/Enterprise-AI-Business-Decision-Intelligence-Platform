AI Prediction Explanation Guide


Sales Prediction:


The sales prediction model analyzes historical sales patterns.

Important factors:

1. Product demand
2. Customer behavior
3. Seasonal trends
4. Discount impact
5. Regional performance


Example:

If sales decrease in a region, the AI checks:

- Customer activity
- Product availability
- Previous sales trend
- Pricing changes



Inventory Prediction:


Inventory AI analyzes:

- Current stock
- Historical demand
- Product movement
- Lead time


The system calculates:

Safety Stock:

Extra inventory required to prevent shortage.


Reorder Point:

The inventory level where new stock should be ordered.



Customer Segmentation:


Customers are grouped according to:

- Purchase frequency
- Spending amount
- Product preference


This helps business teams create targeted campaigns.



Demand Forecasting:


The model identifies:

- Seasonal demand
- Growth patterns
- Future requirements
