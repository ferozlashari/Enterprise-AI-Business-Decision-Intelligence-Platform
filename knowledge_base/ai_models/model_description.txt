Enterprise AI Model Description


The Enterprise AI Business Decision Intelligence Platform uses machine learning models to support business decision making.

Available AI models:

1. Sales Prediction Model

Purpose:
Predict future sales based on historical transaction data.

Algorithm:
Random Forest Regressor
XGBoost Regressor
LightGBM Regressor

Input Features:

- Product Category
- Customer Segment
- Region
- Sales History
- Discount
- Quantity
- Profit
- Date Information


Output:

Predicted Sales Amount


Business Usage:

The model helps managers forecast revenue and identify sales opportunities.



2. Inventory Prediction Model


Purpose:

Predict inventory demand and optimize stock levels.


Algorithms:

Random Forest
XGBoost
LightGBM


Output:

- Future Demand
- Safety Stock
- Reorder Point
- Inventory Risk


Business Usage:

Helps warehouses avoid:

- Overstocking
- Stock shortages
- Supply chain problems



3. Customer Segmentation Model


Purpose:

Group customers based on purchasing behavior.


Algorithm:

K-Means Clustering


Customer Groups:

- High Value Customers
- Regular Customers
- Low Engagement Customers


Business Usage:

Used for marketing strategy and customer retention.



4. Demand Forecasting Model


Purpose:

Predict future product demand.


Techniques:

Time Series Forecasting


Output:

Future demand trends.
