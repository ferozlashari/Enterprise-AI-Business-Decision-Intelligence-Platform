AI Copilot Frequently Asked Questions


Question:
What is AI Copilot?


Answer:
AI Copilot is an enterprise assistant that combines business data, machine learning models and knowledge documents to answer executive questions.



Question:
How does AI Copilot work?


Answer:
It retrieves relevant business knowledge using FAISS, analyzes business data using AI agents and generates responses using Groq LLM.



Question:
What technologies are used?


Answer:

- FastAPI
- Machine Learning
- LangGraph
- FAISS
- RAG
- Groq LLM



Question:
Can AI Copilot predict future sales?


Answer:

Yes. The platform uses forecasting and prediction models for future business analysis.
