Common Business Questions


Question:
Why did sales decrease?


Answer:
Sales decrease can happen because of reduced customer demand, product issues, pricing changes, competition or seasonal changes.



Question:
How can inventory shortage be prevented?


Answer:
Inventory shortage can be prevented using demand forecasting, safety stock calculation and optimized reorder points.



Question:
Which customers are valuable?


Answer:
High value customers are identified using purchasing frequency, spending amount and customer lifetime value.



Question:
What is the biggest business risk?


Answer:
Business risk depends on current conditions including revenue decline, inventory problems and customer churn.
