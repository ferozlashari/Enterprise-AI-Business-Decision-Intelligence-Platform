Enterprise Organization Structure


Executive Management:

CEO:
Responsible for overall business strategy and growth.


CFO:
Responsible for financial performance, revenue and profitability.


COO:
Responsible for operations, supply chain and inventory.


CMO:
Responsible for marketing and customer engagement.


Departments:


Sales Department:

Responsibilities:
- Revenue generation
- Product sales
- Customer acquisition


Inventory Department:

Responsibilities:
- Stock management
- Warehouse operations
- Supply planning


Customer Department:

Responsibilities:
- Customer analysis
- Customer retention
- Customer segmentation


Analytics Department:

Responsibilities:
- Data analysis
- Prediction models
- Business intelligence


AI Department:

Responsibilities:
- Machine learning models
- AI automation
- Decision intelligence
