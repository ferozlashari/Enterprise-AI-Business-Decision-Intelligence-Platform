Enterprise AI Business Decision Intelligence Platform

Company Overview:

This enterprise platform provides artificial intelligence based business intelligence solutions for retail organizations.

The platform analyzes business data from multiple systems including sales, inventory, customer management, finance and operational systems.

The objective is to improve business decisions through predictive analytics, AI recommendations and automated intelligence.


Business Areas:

1. Sales Management
2. Inventory Management
3. Customer Analytics
4. Demand Forecasting
5. Executive Decision Support


AI Capabilities:

- Sales Prediction
- Demand Forecasting
- Inventory Optimization
- Customer Segmentation
- Business Risk Analysis
- AI Executive Copilot


Technology Platform:

Machine Learning:
- Random Forest
- XGBoost
- LightGBM
- Prophet


Artificial Intelligence:
- Retrieval Augmented Generation
- Large Language Models
- Multi-Agent AI System
- LangGraph Workflow


Knowledge Management:

Enterprise documents are stored in a vector database using FAISS.

The AI Copilot retrieves relevant business knowledge and generates executive recommendations.
